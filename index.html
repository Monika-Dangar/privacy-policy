1. Who we are

Indian Off-Road Adventure (“the Game”) is a free-to-play Android game published by Monika Dangar (“we”, “us”). We are the party responsible for the information described in this policy.

Questions or requests about privacy: dangarmonika191023@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Stored on your device. The Game saves your progress locally: coins, distance records, unlocked tracks and vehicles, upgrades, and settings such as sound, music, vibration and pedal layout. We do not run player accounts, and this saved data is not sent to our servers.

Collected through advertising. When your device is online and you use an ad-related feature, Google AdMob may collect certain information so it can show and measure ads:

TypeExamplesUsed for
Identifiers Android advertising ID Showing ads, limiting how often an ad repeats, fraud prevention, and (if you allow it) personalised ads
Device information Device model, operating system version, language, screen size Making sure ads display correctly
Network and approximate location IP address, country or city-level location Choosing relevant ads and detecting fraud
Ad interactions Ads viewed or completed, clicks Giving you your reward coins and measuring ad performance

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address or precise location.

3. Ads and your choices

The Game uses rewarded ads from Google AdMob. You can choose to watch a short ad in exchange for bonus coins. Ads are never required to start or play a run. If you are offline, or no ad is available, the offer is not shown and the rest of the Game works as normal.

Depending on where you live and the choices you make, ads may be:

  • Personalised, based on your advertising ID and your activity across apps and services.
  • Non-personalised, based mainly on general factors such as the Game’s content and your approximate location. These ads still use some device data for limiting repeats, reporting and fraud prevention.

Consent. In regions where it is required (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the Game asks for your consent using Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice later using the Privacy options button in the Game’s Settings screen when it is shown.

Other ways to control ads:

  • On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID, or to turn off ad personalisation.
  • Play with Airplane Mode on. No ad requests are made while the device is offline.

For how Google handles advertising data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not give anyone access to your locally saved game data. Advertising data is processed by Google (Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited) through AdMob, and by the ad partners that take part in Google’s ad systems. Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing, under its own privacy policy.

Data processed by Google may be handled in countries outside your own, including the United States. Google uses safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where the law requires them.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California and other US states), you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information is held about you, and ask for a copy;
  • ask for it to be corrected or deleted;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. Under some US state laws, ad identifiers used for personalised ads may count as “sharing”. You can opt out through the consent options in the Game or your device’s ad settings;
  • not be treated worse for using these rights.

We keep very little personal data ourselves, since saved progress stays on your device and most advertising data is held by Google. To use a right with us, email dangarmonika191023@gmail.com and tell us your country. For data held by Google, you can use Google’s own tools linked above.

If you live in the EEA or the UK and are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

6. Children

The Game is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and the UK, or the age set by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below those ages. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will look into it and take appropriate action.

7. Retention and security

Your saved progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings. Advertising data held by Google is kept according to Google’s own policies.

Communication with Google’s ad services is sent over encrypted connections, and your save data sits inside Android’s app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles data, for example by adding analytics or new ad partners, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above. For significant changes we may also show a notice or ask for consent again in the Game.
